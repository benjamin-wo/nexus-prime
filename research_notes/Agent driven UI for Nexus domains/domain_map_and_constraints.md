## Q1. How do comparable products group life domains, and which domains have the strongest demand? (Recommended domain map)

### Takeaway
Consumer AI use clusters around communication and writing, recommendations and shopping, personal organisation (calendar and tasks), health and fitness advice, and trip planning. Finance is high-value but is the domain where users trust AI least. Super-apps like Revolut grow from a money core into travel and lifestyle, which supports Finance and Travel as Nexus's anchor domains. Health and an "Everyday / Home & Family" domain are the strongest next candidates.

### Cited Findings
- An Aug 2025 Verasight poll of 993 US adults found: 45% use AI to write or edit personal messages, emails or posts; 40% for recommendations (movies, restaurants, gifts); 32% for personal organisation; 32% for health and fitness advice; 28% for entertainment; 20% for trip planning; 19% for calendar management — [Verasight](https://data.verasight.io/ai/at-home-people-mostly-use-ai-for-writing-and-recommendations)
- In Menlo Ventures' 2025 State of Consumer AI survey of 5,000 adults, 34% use AI to help manage childcare, 28% to research topics, and 26% to take and organise notes — [Menlo Ventures](https://www.menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-consumer-ai/)
- Qualtrics reported that "personal and professional support" was the fastest-growing AI use category, nearly doubling from 17% (2024) to 30% (2025) — [Qualtrics](https://www.qualtrics.com/blog/the-top-100-ways-people-are-using-ai-2025/)
- PYMNTS found that everyday planning and commerce-related activities are the most attractive AI-assistant use cases. Only 14% of light users are comfortable using AI for financial or banking tasks. About 44% of non-users would not let an autonomous agent do any personal task in the survey — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3324656); [PYMNTS 2026](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/more-than-60-of-consumers-now-start-daily-tasks-with-ai/)
- 2026 "AI chief of staff" products split by audience. Fambot (launched Sep 2026) targets family logistics for parents. Quill (US$6.5M seed, Feb 2026) grew out of meeting notes and coordinates a professional's AI tools with on-device data. Lindy, Fyxer and Motion focus on one person's inbox and calendar. No startup found clearly bundles finance and health into a chief-of-staff product — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/fambot-introduces-an-ai-chief-of-staff-for-families/); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/quill-launches-sovereign-chief-of-staff-agent-and-raises-6-5m/); [Sentra comparison](https://www.sentra.app/articles/ai-chief-of-staff)
- Revolut, a money super-app, expanded into travel: Stays (hotel booking, launched 2021), Experiences, eSIM in 100+ countries, lounge and fast-track access, and RevPoints loyalty redeemable for travel (36 countries, about 17M participants by end 2025). It had 70M+ customers and 11 product lines each above £100m in revenue — [Globetrender](https://globetrender.com/2021/07/23/digital-banking-app-revolut-launches-travel-booking-service/); [Revolut travel](https://revolut.com/en-NO/travel); [insights4vc](https://insights4vc.substack.com/p/revolut-6b-revenue-70m-customers); [Seedtable RevPoints](https://seedtable.com/products/revpoints)
- Amadeus said in 2026 it is working with Anthropic to connect its travel systems to Claude, a sign that travel content is moving into AI-assistant surfaces — [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/09/03/amadeus-brings-travel-tech-into-claude/); [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/news/technology/amadeus-anthropic-claude-developers-travel-sellers)

### Inferences
- **Recommended domain map** (synthesis, not sourced as a whole):
  1. **Nexus Finance**: day-to-day money (expenses, budgets, bills, subscriptions, cash flow, wallets, salary, receipts, statement import), plus net worth and investments (portfolio, watchlist, trade plans), insurance and CPF/retirement (via SGFinDex where possible), and taxes. This is the existing core. Keep investments as a sub-area, not a separate domain, because SGFinDex and net-worth views join them.
  2. **Nexus Travel**: trips, bookings, itineraries, research, flight status, places and maps, documents (passport and visa expiry), and loyalty points.
  3. **Nexus Health**: activity, sleep, heart rate, weight, workouts (wearables and OS health stores), medical appointments and medications as user-entered records, and health spending. Treat it as **wellness-only** at first (see Q3).
  4. **Nexus Home & Family** (or "Life admin"): household bills already overlap with Finance. This domain adds family calendar and logistics, childcare, groceries and shopping lists, home maintenance, vehicles, and important documents. Supported by demand data (childcare 34%, organisation 32%) and the Fambot launch.
  5. **Nexus Work / Productivity** (cross-cutting, not a panel domain of its own at first): calendar, email, tasks and notes. Every other domain draws on it (receipts come from email, bookings from email, appointments from calendar). Model it as shared infrastructure that Prime uses, with a "Today / Agenda" panel.
  6. Lower priority: Learning, Shopping (could be a Finance sub-view: purchases, price tracking, returns), Social and relationships (birthdays and gifts, could sit in Home & Family).
- Finance has the highest stakes and lowest trust (14% comfortable), so Finance panels should favour transparency (show sources and calculations, user confirmation before actions) over autonomy.

### Gaps
- No primary source found on how Google Gemini, Apple Intelligence/Siri or Microsoft Copilot officially group "domains". Searches were not run for these because of the tool budget.
- No Grab or WeChat service-category data was retrieved (the Revolut search returned nothing on Grab). Grab's Singapore categories (transport, food, mart, GrabPay/finance, insurance) come from background knowledge and are unverified here.
- Singapore-specific consumer AI usage data was not found. All usage statistics above are US or global.

## Q2. Finance in Singapore: SGFinDex, open banking, MAS advice rules, PDPA, and brokerage and market data

### Takeaway
Singapore has **no open-banking mandate**. Bank APIs are voluntary and fintechs report access friction. SGFinDex is the government-backed, Singpass- and consent-based aggregation rail, but the data is consumed through **participating financial planning apps**: in practice bank apps such as OCBC Financial OneView and government tools. No evidence was found that an independent app like Nexus can sign up as a consumer. So Nexus Finance should plan on **user-driven import** (email receipts, statement PDF/CSV import, manual entry, broker CSV exports) and treat SGFinDex as an aspiration that needs a partnership. Showing a user's own data and generic information is low-risk. Personalised product or investment recommendations may become regulated "financial advice" under the FAA **[REVIEW]**.

### Cited Findings
- **SGFinDex mechanics:** It uses an OAuth 2.0 framework through which third-party apps obtain user consent and retrieve data for the user. Singpass is required. SGFinDex itself does not read or store the financial data. Users consent twice: (a) for data contributors (banks, SGX CDP, insurers) and government agencies (via Myinfo) to release data, and (b) for the participating financial planning apps or websites to retrieve it — [SGFinDex API specs](https://specs.api.sgfindex.gov.sg/); [ABS SGFinDex FAQ Phase 2B](https://abs.org.sg/docs/library/sgfindex-frequently-asked-questions.pdf); [SGFinDex FAQ](https://www.sgfindex.gov.sg/faq) (search snippet only; fetch blocked)
- **Consent renewal:** OCBC says consent is extended by one year each time the user syncs data, so users no longer need to renew manually each year if they use SGFinDex within the year — [OCBC Financial OneView](https://www.ocbc.com/personal-banking/digital-banking/financialoneview)
- **Participants and coverage:** SGFinDex was developed by MAS and GovTech/SNDGG with the Association of Banks in Singapore and the Life Insurance Association. It covers bank accounts, credit cards, loans, investments, insurance, CPF and HDB data. Reported counts conflict: "15 contributors" (Georgetown paper, Jan 2025) versus "seven banks, seven insurers and SGX-CDP" (Straits Times via forum). A "200,000+ users" figure dates from about 2022 — [Georgetown Open Banking paper](https://finpolicy.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Open-Banking.pdf); [HardwareZone/ST](https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/banks-insurers-see-more-customers-using-sgfindex-for-financial-planning.7110303/); [Fiskil tracker](https://www.fiskil.com/open-finance-tracker/singapore)
- **Open banking stance:** Regulators encourage but do not mandate open banking. MAS prefers an "organic" approach with no compliance timeline. The 2016 MAS/ABS API Playbook is guidance, not law. DBS's developer portal has 200+ APIs — [Fiskil](https://www.fiskil.com/open-finance-tracker/singapore); [Bloomberg 2018](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2018-04-12/singapore-favors-organic-policy-in-move-toward-open-banking); [Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/the-state-of-open-banking-in-singapore)
- **Access friction:** A July 2025 report describes a gap between Singapore's public open-banking stance and the API-access hurdles fintechs face in practice (headline-level only) — [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2025/07/244828-singapore-fintech-face-api-access-hurdles-report/); see also [Peeka blog "Open banking is broken in Singapore"](https://peekablog.substack.com/p/open-banking-is-broken-in-singapore) (opinion)
- **MAS / Financial Advisers Act:** Anyone carrying on a business of providing financial advisory services needs a licence unless exempt. Generic commentary not tailored to a reader's circumstances is less likely to be financial advice, and MAS encourages "for information only" disclaimers. A tool that collects personal details to shortlist products is a closer case to assess, not an automatic exemption — [MAS SFA/FAA FAQs on exempt persons](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/faqs/cmi/faqs-on-exempt-persons/sfa-and-faa-faqs-on-exempt-persons.pdf); [Conventus Law 2018 summary](https://conventuslaw.com/?p=59682); [MAS digital advisory media release 2017](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2017/facilitating-the-provision-of-digital-advisory-services)
- A vendor blog (less authoritative) says apps that give personalised recommendations or investment advice to Singapore customers generally need a licence, and that a disclaimer does not turn personalised advice into general information — [Arnifi](https://arnifi.com/blog/?p=28469)
- **PDPA breach rules:** Mandatory breach notification to PDPC within 72 hours where there is significant harm or significant scale (500 or more individuals). Significant harm is presumed when a name or national ID is combined with undisclosed financial information (salary, loan history, credit reports), life or health insurance information, or specified medical information — [PDPC Guide on Managing and Notifying Data Breaches](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/Files/PDPC/PDF-Files/Other-Guides/Guide-on-Managing-and-Notifying-Data-Breaches-under-the-PDPA-15-Mar-2021.pdf); [CMS 2021](https://www.cms-lawnow.com/ealerts/2021/02/singapores-mandatory-breach-notification-obligation-takes-effect-immediately); [PDPC enforcement guidelines](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/Files/PDPC/PDF-Files/Advisory-Guidelines/Advisory-Guidelines-on-Enforcement-of-DP-Provisions_1oct2022.pdf)

### Inferences
- **What Finance panels can safely show:** the user's own transactions, budgets, bills, net worth, and portfolio value and P&L from data they supplied; factual market data (prices, with a source and delay noted); scenario calculators driven by the user's inputs. **Higher-risk:** "you should buy X / switch to insurer Y / rebalance to Z" in a way that is personalised and product-specific. That likely crosses into FAA "advising on investment products" territory **[REVIEW]**. The existing "trade plans" feature should be framed as the user's own plan and journal, not system recommendations **[REVIEW]**.
- Because SGFinDex appears limited to participating financial institutions and government apps, a realistic Singapore data strategy is: email receipt parsing (already built), PDF/CSV statement import for DBS/OCBC/UOB and similar, broker CSV or contract-note imports, and manual CPF entry. Partnering with a participating bank is the likely only route to SGFinDex **[REVIEW: confirm with SGFinDex/MAS]**.
- Salary, loan, CPF and insurance data put Nexus in the PDPA's "presumed significant harm" class. Plan for encryption, access logging, and a 72-hour breach runbook.
- **US/EU differences (background knowledge, unverified in this session):** the US has the CFPB §1033 personal financial data rights rule, whose compliance timeline was under litigation and reconsideration in 2025. The EU and UK have PSD2 and UK Open Banking AIS licensing (an account-information service needs authorisation), and the EU FiDA open-finance proposal. An aggregator in the EU/UK needs AISP authorisation or must use a licensed provider (for example Plaid, TrueLayer, Tink) **[REVIEW]**.

### Gaps
- **SGFinDex 2025-2026 status for third parties:** no 2025-2026 announcement was found that opens SGFinDex to non-FI apps. The official FAQ and participant list could not be fetched (`www.sgfindex.gov.sg` blocked). The exact list of "participating financial planning applications" is unknown here.
- **SG brokerage and market data:** no searches were completed on SGX market-data licensing, broker APIs (for example Interactive Brokers, Tiger, moomoo, Saxo, POEMS) or free/paid quote APIs for SGX tickers. This needs follow-up. Redistributing real-time exchange data usually requires an exchange licence.
- The current FAA text and any MAS guidance specific to AI-generated financial content in 2025-2026 were not retrieved. The FAA sources above are older (2017-2018) commentary.
- PDPA consent and purpose rules, the Do Not Call registry, and the 2026 status of PDPC AI advisory guidelines were not separately researched.

## Q3. Health: platform APIs, wearables, Singapore public health data, sensitivity, and the wellness vs medical-device line

### Takeaway
On-device OS health stores (**Apple HealthKit**, **Android Health Connect**) are the most reliable and lowest-cost data sources. Both come with strict store policies: no advertising use, minimum necessary data, and declarations. Cloud wearable APIs have become **less** accessible in 2026. The legacy Fitbit Web API was shut down (2 Nov 2026) in favour of the restricted Google Health API, Garmin reportedly paused new developer applications, and Oura restricted AI processing in its terms (June 2026). Singapore's HealthHub and Healthier SG have **no public third-party API**. Nexus Health should stay a **general-wellness** product (tracking and summarising, no diagnosis or treatment claims) to stay outside HSA and FDA medical-device rules **[REVIEW]**.

### Cited Findings
- **Apple HealthKit:** Apps using HealthKit may show ads but must not use HealthKit data to serve ads. Guideline 5.1.3 bars using health data for advertising, marketing or use-based data mining, and secondary summaries say it bars selling it. Apple tells developers to minimise collection and request permission — [Apple HealthKit: Protecting user privacy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/protecting-user-privacy.md); [Apple HealthKit](https://developer.apple.com/healthkit); [ConductAtlas summary of 5.1.3](https://conductatlas.com/platform/apple/apple-app-store-review-guidelines/provision/CA-P-019078/health-and-homekit-api-data-prohibited-for-marketing-or-advertising/)
- **Android Health Connect:** Since 31 Aug 2024 access is declared through the Play Console Health apps declaration (existing apps had to resubmit by 22 Jan 2025). Apps that are not primarily health apps but access health data are still in scope. Health Connect data is "personal and sensitive user data". Apps must request the minimum data types with a valid use case for each, and must refile when data types change. An Oct 2025 policy update incorporated EU MDCG medical-device-app guidance — [Google Play Health Connect policy FAQs](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134); [Android: Publish your health app](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/publish)
- **Fitbit → Google Health API:** Legacy Fitbit Web API support ended 30 Sep 2026. The API and Fitbit OAuth endpoints turn off on 2 Nov 2026 and return HTTP 410 after that. One mirror lists 30 Oct, and Thryve said 30 Sep. Users must re-consent through Google OAuth. All Google Health API scopes are "Restricted" and require a privacy and security review. In Oct 2026 Google was reportedly "not onboarding new projects at this time". On-device Android apps are steered to Health Connect — [Google Health API: About](https://developers.google.com/health/about); [Fitbit dev blog Sep 2025](https://dev.fitbit.com/blog/2025-09-26-important-updates-to-the-fitbit-web-apis/); [Sahha, 29 Oct 2026](https://sahha.ai/blog/fitbit-api-sunset-migration/); [Thryve](https://www.thryve.health/blog/fitbit-shutdown-september-2026-migration-options)
- **Garmin:** The Connect Developer Program is free for approved **business** developers (a legal entity is required, with Starter then Enterprise tiers, and it is not self-serve). A Sep 2026 Terra blog and forum posts say new applications are paused. Garmin's own pages do not mention a pause, so the sources conflict — [Garmin Health API](https://developer.garmin.com/gc-developer-program/health-api); [Garmin program overview](https://developer.garmin.com/gc-developer-program/overview); [Terra: Garmin pause](https://tryterra.co/blog/garmin-connect-developer-program-pause)
- **Oura API v2:** Covers sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate and workouts. Gen3 and newer users need an active Oura Membership. Apps are limited to 10 users until Oura approves them. Personal access tokens were deprecated Dec 2025. Per Terra, Oura's agreement effective 8 Jun 2026 restricts AI processing, downstream access and monetisation (third-party source; verify against Oura's own terms) — [Terra: Oura API & AI apps](https://tryterra.co/blog/oura-api-ai-apps-access-permissions-commercial-restrictions)
- **Singapore HealthHub / Healthier SG:** No public developer API was found. IHiS (now Synapxe) announced an API-first strategy with Google Cloud Apigee and Accenture in Feb 2023, describing secure API access for trusted third-party developers, but nothing specific to HealthHub. MOH (Mar 2025) plans to merge HealthHub and the three cluster apps into one app by 2027. The Healthy 365 privacy statement covers sharing with HPB's own partners only — [Google Cloud press, IHiS](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2023-02-14-IHiS-Advances-API-First-Strategy-with-Google-Cloud-and-Accenture-to-Drive-Open-and-Secure-Healthcare-Ecosystem-Innovation); [MOH "front door"](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/integrating-public-health-apps-into-one--front-door--with-common-design-and-interoperability/); [Healthy 365 privacy statement](https://www.healthhub.sg/programmes/healthyliving/privacy-statement)
- **HSA (Singapore):** HSA's "Regulatory Guidelines for Software Medical Devices – A Life Cycle Approach" (GL-04; R2 April 2022, R3 about March 2024, with ML devices covered per a May 2026 industry briefing) governs software medical devices. APACMed's summary of HSA's approach says software marketed as "wellness" can still be a regulated device if it performs a medical function (for example heart-rate monitoring for a medical purpose) — [HSA GL-04 R2](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/docs/default-source/hprg-mdb/guidance-documents-for-medical-devices/regulatory-guidelines-for-software-medical-devices---a-life-cycle-approach_r2-(2022-apr)-pub.pdf); [HSA industry briefing May 2026](https://file.go.gov.sg/industry-briefing-20may2026.pdf); [APACMed](https://apacmed.org/content/uploads/2021/01/APACMed-Digital-Health-Regulation-in-APAC.pdf)
- **FDA (US):** The FDA updated its General Wellness and Clinical Decision Support guidances in January 2026. They are nonbinding, and higher-risk products (implants, lasers, radiation) fall outside general wellness. The SaMD Clinical Evaluation guidance was withdrawn on 7 Jan 2026 — [Faegre Drinker, Jan 2026](https://faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/1/key-updates-in-fdas-2026-general-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-guidance); [Exponent, Jan 2026](https://www.exponent.com/article/fda-clarifies-oversight-wearables-and-cds-software); [Emergo by UL](https://emergobyul.com/news/fda-updates-broaden-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-devices-under-enforcement)
- **Australia (comparator):** TGA Exclusion 14B covers consumer software that promotes general wellness by non-invasive measurement. Every feature of a multi-function product must meet the criteria, and the exclusion fails if the software is intended for a serious disease or condition — [TGA](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/understanding-general-health-or-wellness-software-exclusion)
- **PDPA:** Specified medical information and life or health insurance data trigger the presumption of "significant harm" in breach notification (see Q2) — [PDPC breach guide](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/Files/PDPC/PDF-Files/Other-Guides/Guide-on-Managing-and-Notifying-Data-Breaches-under-the-PDPA-15-Mar-2021.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Panel-design implications:** Health panels can show trends (steps, sleep, resting HR, weight), goals, workout logs, user-entered appointments and medications, and health spending. Avoid outputs that interpret readings diagnostically ("your HRV suggests atrial fibrillation"), medication dosing, or condition-specific treatment guidance. These are what push software toward HSA Class A-D or FDA device status **[REVIEW]**. The "every feature" rule (TGA) suggests one medical-claim feature can taint the whole product.
- Nexus is web-first (Python backend plus `web/`). HealthKit has no web or server API: it needs a native iOS app or companion. Health Connect is likewise on-device Android. A web-only Nexus would need a companion mobile app, user CSV/XML exports (Apple Health export), or an aggregator (Terra, Thryve, Sahha, Open Wearables). Aggregators inherit each vendor's restrictions, including Oura's AI terms **[verify]**.
- Mixing health data with Finance (health spending) or using it in LLM prompts needs explicit consent and purpose limitation. If health data leaves the device to an LLM provider, check App Store 5.1.3 and Health Connect "minimum use" rules plus the Oura and Google Health terms **[REVIEW]**.
- **EU/US:** health data is a GDPR Article 9 special category (explicit consent). In the US, HIPAA usually does not apply to direct-to-consumer apps, but the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state laws (for example Washington's My Health My Data Act) do (background knowledge, not verified this session) **[REVIEW]**.

### Gaps
- The exact text of HSA GL-04 R3 on wellness and lifestyle exclusions was not read. The HSA position comes from a secondary 2021 APACMed summary.
- Samsung Health, Whoop, Withings and Strava API terms were not researched.
- PDPC guidance treating health data as "sensitive" beyond breach notification (for example in the Advisory Guidelines on Key Concepts) was not retrieved.

## Q4. Travel: booking and airline data, flight status, maps and places APIs and their costs

### Takeaway
The easiest developer travel API, **Amadeus Self-Service, closed on 17 Jul 2026**, leaving enterprise contracts or other vendors. For a personal assistant, **email parsing of confirmations** (Nexus already parses receipts) plus a metered **flight-status API** and **Google Places (New)** is the practical stack. Google Maps moved from a US$200 credit to per-SKU free caps in March 2025, so costs depend on which "Pro/Enterprise" place fields panels request.

### Cited Findings
- **Amadeus:** In Feb 2026 Amadeus confirmed it is decommissioning the Self-Service part of Amadeus for Developers. Keys were disabled and the portal closed on 17 Jul 2026, and the amadeus4dev GitHub org was archived. The Enterprise portal is unaffected, and self-service users were told to contact account managers. No reason or pricing was given. Amadeus is now integrating with Claude for developers and travel sellers — [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/amadeus-shut-down-self-service-apis-portal-developers); [Amadeus statement](https://amadeus.com/en/industry-messaging/statement-regarding-amadeus-for-developers-portal); [Skift Sep 2026](https://skift.com/2026/09/03/amadeus-brings-travel-tech-into-claude/)
- **Flight status:** FlightAware AeroAPI is billed per query, with about US$5/month of free credit. Aviationstack has a free tier of about 100 requests/month and paid plans from about US$45-50/month for 10k requests. AeroDataBox offers about 600 free units/month. These are from comparison sites with conflicting figures, so verify on vendor pages — [FlightAware AeroAPI](https://flightaware.com/aeroapi); [Geekflare 2026](https://geekflare.com/flight-data-api/); [Thunderbit 2026](https://thunderbit.com/ja/blog/best-flight-api-with-free-tiers)
- **Google Maps Platform (from 1 Mar 2025):** The US$200 monthly credit was replaced with per-SKU monthly free caps: Essentials 10,000, Pro 5,000, Enterprise 1,000 billable events. Places, Directions and Distance Matrix were made "legacy" in favour of Places API (New) and Routes API. Nearby Search, Place Details and Text Search are Pro SKUs. Optional subscriptions: Starter US$100/month (50k calls), Essentials US$275 (100k), Pro US$1,200 (250k) — [Google: March 2025 changes](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/march-2025); [Google pricing overview](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing/overview/); [Google billing FAQ](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/faq)

### Inferences
- Itinerary and booking panels should be populated mainly from parsed confirmation emails (airline, hotel, Klook, Agoda, Booking.com), with a flight-status API used only for upcoming legs to keep metered costs low.
- Use field masks on Places (New) so place cards request Essentials-tier fields where possible. Requesting ratings, reviews or opening hours moves calls into the Pro/Enterprise caps.
- Travel booking (selling tickets) would bring in travel-agent licensing (Singapore Travel Agents Act / STB licence) and payment rules. Keeping Nexus to planning, tracking and deep links avoids this **[REVIEW]**.

### Gaps
- Alternatives to Amadeus for flight and hotel shopping (Duffel, Sabre, Travelport, Kiwi Tequila, Booking/Expedia affiliate APIs) were not researched.
- Mapbox, OneMap (Singapore's free government map API) and Apple MapKit JS pricing were not researched. OneMap is likely relevant for Singapore addresses (background knowledge).
- Gmail API restricted-scope verification (CASA security assessment) for reading users' email in production was not researched. It is a significant constraint for email-parsing travel and finance features **[REVIEW]**.

## Q5. Cross-domain links that make multi-domain panels valuable

### Takeaway
The highest-value panels sit where the money ledger meets another domain's events: **trip budget and spend** (Travel × Finance), **health spending and insurance claims** (Health × Finance), and a **calendar/agenda spine** that schedules bills, flights and appointments. Revolut's model (money core → travel bookings → loyalty points) shows these joins in a shipped product.

### Cited Findings
- Revolut links money and travel through Stays bookings with cashback (more than £1.5M paid out), travel benefits, and RevPoints redeemable for air miles and hotels — [Revolut travel](https://revolut.com/en-NO/travel); [Seedtable RevPoints](https://seedtable.com/products/revpoints)
- SGFinDex already joins finance with insurance, CPF and HDB data, so a net-worth or retirement view is inherently cross-domain (Finance × Housing × Insurance) — [Fiskil](https://www.fiskil.com/open-finance-tracker/singapore)
- In the US, the IRS issued an FAQ that a general wellness or fitness wearable is not a medical expense merely because it tracks health. Subscriptions count only if bought primarily for a medical purpose. This is an example of a Health × Finance categorisation rule (US-specific) — [Thomson Reuters Tax](https://tax.thomsonreuters.com/news/new-irs-medical-expenses-faq-addresses-wearable-devices/)
- Calendar management (19%) and personal organisation (32%) are common assistant uses, which supports a shared agenda layer — [Verasight](https://data.verasight.io/ai/at-home-people-mostly-use-ai-for-writing-and-recommendations)

### Inferences
- Suggested cross-domain panels:
  - **Trip budget**: planned versus actual spend by trip, FX in SGD, card or wallet used, and points earned (Travel × Finance).
  - **Upcoming obligations**: bills, subscription renewals, flights, appointments and passport expiry on one timeline (Work/Calendar × Finance × Travel × Health).
  - **Health spending**: gym, wearables, clinic visits, insurance premiums and claims, and MediSave/CPF where available (Health × Finance).
  - **Household**: shared bills, groceries and family calendar (Home & Family × Finance).
  - **Travel health**: sleep and activity during trips, jet-lag, vaccination and medication reminders (Travel × Health), kept within wellness framing.
- Architecturally, each domain should expose typed entities (Transaction, Trip, Booking, Appointment, Metric) with shared keys (date range, location, merchant, person). Prime can then compose multi-domain panels. Consent scopes should be per domain so health data is not pulled into finance views without the user opting in (PDPA purpose limitation) **[REVIEW]**.

### Gaps
- No market research was found that quantifies demand for specific cross-domain features (for example trip budgeting) in an assistant context.
- Singapore-specific Health × Finance rules (MediSave withdrawal limits, Integrated Shield claims data access) were not researched.
