One assistant. A whole set of apps behind it.

You never open them. Prime does, the moment your question needs one, and puts away what you don't.

“Can I afford Japan in March?” … then: “And April?”

Prime keeps the thread. The view in front of you changes to April. There are no menus to dig through and no tabs to find. You talk, and the screen follows.

How it works

  1. 01Say itTalk or type, on the web, in Telegram or on your phone. Half a sentence is fine.
  2. 02Prime answersA short, plain answer, and only what you asked for.
  3. 03The right screen opensThe view that answers you, built from your own data. Ask a follow-up and it updates in place.
