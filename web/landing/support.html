Support

Get help

Email hello@nexus-prime.app. Tell us what you were doing and what you expected.

If you use Nexus through Telegram, you can also message the Nexus bot.

Report a problem

Write to hello@nexus-prime.app with what happened, roughly when, and which device or app you used (web, Telegram or iPhone). Please do not send passwords. A screenshot helps, but cover anything private first.

Export or delete your data

Both are in the app: open Settings, then Your data. Export gives you a zip of everything we hold about you. Delete removes your account and all your data and cannot be undone. See the privacy page for details.

Sign in

Go to the sign-in page. To look around first, try the demo; it uses made-up data and is erased after 24 hours.
